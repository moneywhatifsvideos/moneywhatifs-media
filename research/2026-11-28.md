## What's working out there

1. **"At every level" and "average person" titles are the finance outliers right now.**
   - Dark Ledger (30K subs): "How Banks Treat You at Every Level of Wealth — $0 to $25M" 1.1M; "Your Life at Every Salary Level" 300K.
   - OutlierKit's top finance outlier title type is "Shocking money stats about the average person": Everyday Finance's version reached 120x its channel average, and Humphrey Yang's "10 Jaw-Dropping Money Stats of the Average Person (2026)" got 1.4M.
   - This builds on last week's "Where do you rank?" finding.
   - Sources: outlierkit.com/channel/darkledger13, outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-finance-niche-creators/
2. **POV money identity titles keep producing outliers.**
   - POV Finance (28.7K subs): "POV You Adopted the Old Money Mindset" 667K; "POV: You Chose Wealth Over Looking Rich" 369K.
   - Dark Ledger: "POV: You Became Richer Than Your Friends — Then Things Got Weird" 199K.
   - Source: outlierkit.com/channel/povfinanceus
3. **Sequels and series work for stick figures, and YouTube now supports series directly.**
   - Stickify (131K subs, stick-figure self-help) reached 429K with a "PART 2" sequel.
   - YouTube began rolling out **Shorts Series** on 23 Sep (seasons, episodes, sequential playback).
   - The same story told long-form flopped: Sketchinance's "Two Brothers. Same Salary. $740,000 Apart at 50" got 828 views. As a short, numbered series it is untested.
   - Sources: outlierkit.com/channel/stickifyyy, digitaltrends.com (Shorts Series), vidiq.com (Sketchinance)
4. **YouTube now rewards originality (1 Oct) and is changing Shorts money from 1 Feb 2027.**
   - Shorts recommendations now favour original content. Edits made only from templates count as unoriginal.
   - From 1 Feb, YPP entry through Shorts needs 20M views in 90 days, and channels must keep 10M per 90 days to stay monetised.
   - Shorts RPM is about $0.03–0.10. Life Doodles has 7.3B views but only an estimated ~$1.1K of ad revenue.
   - So for income, TikTok Creator Rewards (over 1 minute) and later long-form matter more than YouTube Shorts ads.
   - Sources: androidauthority.com, outlierkit.com/resources/youtube-algorithm-updates/, cartoonbrew.com, virlo.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-monetization, vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/@lifedoodlesshort/
5. **TikTok now lets creators add or block search keywords on each video, and shows AI summaries in search.**
   - Search keywords matter more than ever. Our "#fyp" captions with no keyword waste this.
   - Source: metricool.com/tiktok-news
6. **New channels have to play the volume game, and outliers come early.**
   - vidIQ (Jul 2026): a new channel's typical Short gets about 1.3K views, and that doesn't improve over its first 10.
   - The best of the first 10 gets about 6.5x that, and 48% of channels had one Short pass 10K within their first 10.
   - Our G06 (888 on TikTok) is our outlier, so we should make more like it.
   - Source: vidiq.com/research/youtube-first-10-videos-study/
7. **Christmas and January money numbers are everywhere this week.**

   - Nationwide: £103 on gifts for the closest loved one; 45–54s spend £119 against £87 for 18–24s; Black Friday weekend forecast at 43.95M transactions (+9%).

## Recommendations (ranked; all PROPOSED)

1. **PROPOSED: Keyword-first captions and on-screen text, no "#fyp", and a specific question at the end.**
   - Make the first words of the caption the search phrase (e.g. "UK house prices halve: what happens to your mortgage?").
   - Show the same phrase on screen within 2 seconds.
   - Use 3–5 specific hashtags (#ukhouseprices, not #fyp).
   - Add TikTok's new search keywords to each post.
   - *Why:* 14 TikToks drew 0 comments from vague questions, TikTok now lets us set keywords, and search shows AI summaries.
   - *Impact:* medium-high. *Effort:* low (caption template in the pack only).
2. **PROPOSED: Make more of what's already winning: shocks to "your home / your debt" What Ifs, and personal-hook data videos ("Where do you live?").**
   - Aim for at least half of next week's What Ifs to be housing, mortgage or debt shocks, tied to falling prices, the 17 Dec rate decision and the January cap.
   - *Why:* G06 got 888 and G05 485, against a median of about 185. D02, with a personal "where do you live" hook, was our best data video. vidIQ says early outliers are the signal to follow.
   - *Impact:* high. *Effort:* low (topic choice).
3. **PROPOSED: A UK "At every level / where do you rank" series.** Examples: "Your Christmas at every salary level", "How the bank treats you at every savings level", "10 shocking money stats about the average Brit".
   - *Why:* Dark Ledger got 1.1M and 300K, the "average person" format got 1.4M and 120x, and Damien got 831K last week. It fits our bar ranking.
   - *Impact:* high. *Effort:* low-medium.
4. **PROPOSED: A numbered stick-figure series with the same two characters (e.g. "Two flatmates, same salary — Part 1, 2, 3…"), added to a YouTube Shorts Series.**
   - *Why:* Stickify's Part 2 got 429K, POV Finance 667K, Money Life POV 2.4M from last week, and YouTube now plays series in order.
   - *Impact:* high. *Effort:* medium (script pattern; existing engine).
5. **PROPOSED: Vary the look of each video (backgrounds, colours, props, music), not just the text.**
   - *Why:* since 1 Oct YouTube's recommendations favour original content and mark template-only edits as unoriginal, and our YouTube views fell after 21 Nov.
   - *Impact:* medium (risk reduction). *Effort:* medium (engine; only if approved).
