## What's working out there

1. **"Where do you rank?" UK benchmark titles are the strongest UK money pattern.**
   - Damien Talks Money: "Average UK Savings By Age: How Do You Compare?" has 831K views, his biggest recent hit. "Signs You're Doing Well Financially" has 1.2M.
   - British Finance with Jack (10.8K subs): "How Much House Can You Afford in the UK? (By Salary)" 133K; "Top 10% in the UK" 56K; "Average UK Net Worth At Every Age" 42K.
   - Humphrey Yang: "How Much Car Can You Really Afford? (By Salary)" 2.9M.
   - Mostly long-form, but the hook works for a 63s bar ranking. Sources: outlierkit.com/channel/damientalksmoney, /britishfinancewithjack, /humphrey
2. **A recurring "POV: you…" money character grows fast.**
   - Money Life POV is a new channel (about 0.1 years old) with 2.4M views from 43 videos.
   - "POV: You Became the Rich Friend…" 344K; "POV: You Pay Off Your House While Everyone Else Still Has a Mortgage" 244K; "POV: You Keep the Same Car for 10 Years…" 220K.
   - This fits our stick figure directly. Source: outlierkit.com/channel/moneylifepov
3. **Faceless animated "why / what if" explainers scale.**
   - Explains101: "Why Every Country Is in Debt?" 2.7M; "Why Can't We Just Print More Money?" 1.0M; "What If Inflation Goes Negative?" 623K.
   - How Money Works: "If Nobody Can Afford A Home… Who's Going To Buy Them?" 4.0M.
   - These are big-economy questions asked in plain words. Sources: outlierkit.com/channel/explains101, /howmoneyworks
4. **"Then vs now" UK prices go viral, but get fact-checked.**
   - 2000 vs 2026 price posts (Freddo, pint, house) spread across platforms. Full Fact rated them "missing context": wrong 2026 figures, and earnings actually rose about 110%, not 90%.
   - A 2001 Morrisons receipt also went viral (LADbible, Oct 2025).
   - Our opening: then-vs-now with ONS sources, and "fact-checked" as the hook. Sources: fullfact.org, ladbible.com (links below)
5. **UK vs USA salary shock keeps coming back.** A US TikToker reacting to "is £30K a good UK salary?" made LADbible (May 2025; no view count). This matches Money Stories' best format, as Archie noted.
6. **Hook and retention rules from Shorts studies.**
   - vidIQ: Shorts around 13s or 60s perform best, from a study of 35B views. Watch "Viewed vs Swiped Away" in YouTube Studio.
   - Jenny Hoyos: make it work on mute; hint at the ending early; link beats with "but / so"; give series similar titles so YouTube chains them.
   - Joe Sbiti: viral Shorts usually keep over 75% of viewers past the swipe.
7. **TikTok search is a real traffic source.**
   - Metricool: put the keyword in the caption's first line, in on-screen text within 3 seconds, spoken aloud, and in the file name. Use 1–5 specific hashtags.
   - Questions in captions get about 26% more comments. Asking for likes cuts likes by about 60%. About 96% of reach comes in the first 10 days.
   - One of our TikTok captions opens with "#fyp", which uses up that keyword slot.
8. **Platform rules that affect us.**
   - **YouTube:**
     - The "inauthentic / mass-produced" policy (since 15 Jul 2025) puts templated look-alike AI videos at risk.
     - A Short over 1 minute with any active Content ID claim is blocked worldwide. That matters for our 63s+ videos: use only cleared music.
     - From 1 Feb 2027, joining YPP through Shorts needs 20M Shorts views in 90 days.
   - **TikTok:**
     - Since Nov 2025, viewers can turn down AI content with a slider. AI labels matter.
     - Creator Rewards needs 10K followers and 100K views in 30 days, and only videos over 1 minute count, which we already meet.
     - Reported pay is about £0.22–£0.59 per 1,000 views, and has been cut suddenly before.

## Recommendations (ranked; none adopted yet)

1. **PROPOSED: Start a "Where do you rank?" UK series ("By salary", "By age", "Top 10%") for Data and Money mindset slots.**
   - *Why:* this is the clearest repeatable winner in UK money (831K, 133K, 2.9M examples above), and viewers compare themselves and comment.
   - *Impact:* high.
   - *Effort:* low. Our bar race and ranking already work; ONS ASHE 2026 has pay by occupation and percentile.
2. **PROPOSED: Search-first hooks and captions.**
   - On-screen keyword text in the first 1–2 seconds, spoken keyword in line 1 of the voiceover, and the keyword as the first words of the caption (never "#fyp" first).
   - End captions with a question, never "like this".
   - Use 3–5 specific hashtags.
   - *Why:* Metricool TikTok SEO data and the vidIQ / Jenny Hoyos retention rules.
   - *Impact:* medium-high.
   - *Effort:* low (script and caption template only).
3. **PROPOSED: A recurring "POV: you…" stick-figure series.**
   - Same character, same title template ("POV: You … while everyone else …"), numbered parts.
   - *Why:* Money Life POV got 2.4M views in about 5 weeks with exactly this. Series titles help YouTube chain videos.
   - *Impact:* high.
   - *Effort:* medium (new script pattern, existing engine).
4. **PROPOSED: Publish within 48h of official releases, with "fact-checked: source ONS / Ofgem" on screen.**
   - Targets: price cap 25 Nov, CPI 16 Dec, BoE 17 Dec, then-vs-now prices.
   - *Why:* Full Fact shows then-vs-now goes viral and is often wrong, so being correct and sourced is our edge. Trending slots exist already.
   - *Impact:* medium.
   - *Effort:* low-medium.
5. **PROPOSED: Vary visuals per video (backgrounds, colour accents, scene props, music), not just the text.**
   - *Why:*
     - YouTube's mass-produced content policy.
     - TikTok's AI-content slider.
     - Shorts over 60s are blocked if they have any Content ID music claim.
   - Protects reach and future monetisation.
   - *Impact:* medium (risk reduction).
   - *Effort:* medium (engine work; only if approved).

Reply "approve 1, 3" in the chat to adopt any of these.
